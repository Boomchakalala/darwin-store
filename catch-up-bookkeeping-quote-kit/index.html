Questions

Does it tell me what hourly rate to charge?

No. It is currency-neutral and uses your own rate (R). It turns that rate into a defensible fixed fee based on volume, account-months and messiness, and includes a check that your catch-up price per month is at least 1.5x your ongoing monthly fee.

What format is it?

A single Markdown document. It includes tables, a formula, a worked example, paste-ready proposal clauses, email scripts and a checklist. It opens in any text editor, Notion or Obsidian.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. It covers scoping, pricing and client communication for bookkeeping work. The scope template explicitly excludes tax filings, and you should adapt the clauses to your own engagement terms.

Will the default throughput numbers fit me?

They are starting points. The kit shows you how to time one sample month and replace the defaults with your own transactions-per-hour figure.
