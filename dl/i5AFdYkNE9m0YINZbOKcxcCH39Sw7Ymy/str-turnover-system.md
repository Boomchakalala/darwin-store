# The STR Turnover System

Cleaner checklists, photo proof, par levels, damage protocol and message scripts for short-term rental hosts who use cleaners, or who clean themselves and want to stop forgetting things.

## Set it up in 30 minutes

1. Paste Section 2 (Cleaner Checklist) into whatever your cleaner already uses: a shared Google Doc, a Turno or Breezeway checklist, or a pinned WhatsApp message.
2. Fill in the par sheet in Section 4 for your unit and write the numbers on the inside of your owner's closet door.
3. Send your cleaner Template 1 (Section 8). It sets the photo rule: no final photos, no payment approval.
4. Time your next 3 turnovers and correct the estimates in Section 1 for your unit.

## 1. Turnover timing

Typical starting estimates for one experienced cleaner, with linens swapped from clean stock (not washed on site). Replace them with your own averages after 3 turnovers.

| Unit | Cleaner-hours | Add if washing + drying on site |
|---|---|---|
| Studio | 1.25–1.75 h | +1.0 h |
| 1BR / 1BA | 1.75–2.25 h | +1.25 h |
| 2BR / 1–2BA | 2.5–3.25 h | +1.5 h |
| 3BR / 2BA | 3.5–4.5 h | +2.0 h |
| 4BR+ | 5 h+ (use 2 cleaners) | +2.5 h |

Adders: hot tub or pool check +30 min. Pet stay +30–45 min. Stay longer than 7 nights +30–60 min. Grill cleaning +15 min. Group of 6+ guests +20%.

**Same-day window rule.** Window = check-in time − checkout time − 30 min buffer (late leavers, traffic, keys). Then:

- If window ≥ estimated time × 1.3: safe. You may grant small early check-in or late checkout requests.
- If window is between 1.0× and 1.3×: safe only with your regular cleaner. Decline all early and late requests.
- If window < 1.0×: add a second cleaner or block the night. Two cleaners do not halve the time; divide by 1.7.

Example: checkout 11:00, check-in 15:00, buffer 30 min → window 3.5 h. A 2BR with on-site laundry = 3.25 + 1.5 = 4.75 h. That is under 1.0×, so either send linens out (back to 3.25 h, now 1.08×), add a second cleaner (4.75 ÷ 1.7 = 2.8 h, now 1.25×), or move check-in to 16:00.

**Late checkout decision**

| Next guest arrives same day? | Window after late checkout ≥ 1.3× estimate? | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| No | — | Yes (charge or free, your policy) |
| Yes | Yes | Yes, max 1 hour |
| Yes | No | No. Offer luggage drop instead (Template 4) |

## 2. Cleaner checklist (order matters)

The sequence below avoids the two biggest time-wasters: waiting on laundry and re-cleaning floors.

### A. Arrival, before touching anything (5–8 min)

- [ ] Record a 60–90 second as-found video walking every room, opening the fridge, oven and bathroom bins.
- [ ] Check for: damage, missing items (use the inventory list), smoke smell, pet hair or evidence, extra guests (extra used towels, bedding on the sofa), stains on mattress or sofa.
- [ ] If anything is off: photograph it close-up and wide, send to host within 15 minutes using the Template 2 format. Do not clean that spot until the host replies or 30 minutes pass.

### B. Start the slow tasks first (10 min)

- [ ] Strip all beds and check each mattress protector (stained → protector goes in the wash, photograph the mattress).
- [ ] Start laundry if on site: sheets first, towels second.
- [ ] Load and run the dishwasher with whatever guests left.
- [ ] Spray toilets, shower, tub and sinks with cleaner and leave it to dwell.
- [ ] Empty every bin into one bag, put in new liners.

### C. Kitchen

- [ ] Clear fridge and freezer of all guest food (sealed items: your policy; default is discard).
- [ ] Wipe fridge shelves and door seals.
- [ ] Inside microwave, quick oven check (wipe spills), stovetop, backsplash.
- [ ] Counters, cabinet fronts, handles, kettle and coffee maker exterior; empty coffee grounds or pods and the drip tray.
- [ ] Unload dishwasher; inspect each item for residue, re-wash by hand if needed; count glasses, mugs and cutlery against the inventory.
- [ ] Sink scrubbed and dried, tap polished.
- [ ] Restock to par: dish soap, sponge, dishwasher tablets, paper towel, coffee and tea, salt, pepper, oil.

### D. Bathrooms

- [ ] Scrub what was sprayed: toilet (including the base and behind the seat hinges), shower walls, glass, tub, sink.
- [ ] Hair check: drain, floor corners, behind the toilet, shower floor. Hair is the most common cleanliness complaint. Check twice.
- [ ] Mirror streak-free; check it from an angle.
- [ ] Restock to par: toilet paper, hand soap, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, towels set out per guest count.
- [ ] Bin empty with liner, floor mopped last.

### E. Bedrooms

- [ ] Make beds with fresh linen; check sheets for stains and holes in good light before putting them on.
- [ ] Under-bed and behind-nightstand check for guest items and rubbish.
- [ ] Closets: hangers in place (count), drawers empty, spare blanket and pillow present.
- [ ] Dust surfaces, lamps, headboard.
- [ ] Test lamps and USB chargers.

### F. Living areas

- [ ] Remove cushions, vacuum sofa seams, check under cushions.
- [ ] Dust TV and shelves; remotes wiped and placed in the same spot; check the TV turns on.
- [ ] Wipe light switches, door handles, remotes, thermostat.
- [ ] Clean hand-height smudges on windows and patio door glass.

### G. Outdoor (if any)

- [ ] Sweep entry and patio, wipe outdoor table, empty ashtrays if smoking is allowed outside, check grill is clean and gas level.
- [ ] Hot tub: test chemistry, cover on and latched.

### H. Floors and exit

- [ ] Vacuum all floors, then mop hard floors, working toward the exit.
- [ ] Final walk with the Guest-Eye Pass (below).
- [ ] Take the final photo set (Section 3).
- [ ] Thermostat to your standard setting, lights off except the entry lamp, windows locked, door locked, key or lockbox reset.
- [ ] Send the Turnover Report (Section 9).

### Guest-Eye Pass (3 minutes, from the doorway of each room)

1. Is there anything on the floor?
2. Are all bins empty?
3. Is every bed tight with no visible wrinkles on the top cover?
4. Any hair visible in the bathroom from standing height?
5. Do mirrors and glass show streaks when you look sideways?
6. Do towels match and sit folded the same way?
7. Smell: neutral, not perfume covering a bad smell?
8. Are remotes, Wi-Fi card and house manual in their spots?
9. Is the welcome item (if you use one) placed?
10. Are all lights working?

## 3. Photo proof: the 14-shot set

Final photos protect three people: the host in damage disputes, the cleaner when a guest complains unfairly, and the next guest. The set below is for a 1BR/1BA. For each extra bedroom repeat shots 9–10; for each extra bathroom repeat shots 11–13.

| # | Shot | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front door from inside, lock visible | Entry clean, lock state |
| 2 | Living room wide from doorway | Overall condition |
| 3 | Sofa straight on, cushions in place | No stains, cushions present |
| 4 | TV wall with remotes visible | Electronics present |
| 5 | Kitchen wide | Counters clear |
| 6 | Open fridge | Empty and clean |
| 7 | Open microwave + stovetop | No residue |
| 8 | Open cutlery drawer + glasses cabinet | Inventory present |
| 9 | Bed straight on, pillows set | Linens fresh, made correctly |
| 10 | Floor under and beside bed | No items left |
| 11 | Toilet, seat down | Clean, paper stocked |
| 12 | Shower or tub with drain visible | No hair |
| 13 | Sink + mirror | No streaks, soap stocked |
| 14 | Towel setup | Correct count for guests |

Rules: same angle every time (put a small tape dot on the floor where to stand), landscape orientation, lights on, original files sent so timestamps are kept (no screenshots). Store by date in one shared album per property.

## 4. Par levels and restocking

### Linens

- **Sheet sets:** 3 per bed (on the bed, in the wash, spare). Use 4 per bed if your laundry service takes longer than 24 hours or you have frequent same-day turnovers.
- **Towels per guest of max capacity:** 1 bath towel, 1 hand towel, 1 washcloth set out, plus 1 extra bath towel per 2 guests. Stock 3× that total.
- **Mattress protectors:** 2 per bed. **Pillow protectors:** 2 per pillow.
- **Buy all-white linens** in one style per bed size. Mixed sets slow cleaners down and hide shortages.

Example: 2BR sleeping 4, with 1 queen + 2 twins. Sheet sets: 3 queen + 6 twin. Towels set out: 4 bath + 2 extra = 6 bath, 4 hand, 4 washcloth. Stock: 18 bath, 12 hand, 12 washcloth.

### Consumables: starter quantities

Usage assumes 2 guests for 3 nights. Scale with guest-nights.

| Item | Typical use per turnover | Set out at check-in | Backstock (owner closet) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Toilet paper | 3–4 rolls | 2 per toilet + 2 in cabinet | 12 rolls |
| Paper towels | 1 roll | 1 + 1 spare | 6 rolls |
| Dishwasher tablets | 2–3 | 6 | 30 |
| Dish soap | ~10% of bottle | bottle at least 1/2 full | 2 bottles |
| Hand soap | ~15% of bottle | refill when below 1/2 | 2 refills |
| Shampoo, conditioner, body wash (dispensers) | ~10% each | refill when below 1/2 | 1 litre each |
| Coffee pods or ground coffee | 12 pods or ~100 g | 12–16 pods | 40 pods |
| Tea bags | 4–6 | 10 | 40 |
| Kitchen trash bags | 3–5 | 1 roll in cabinet | 2 rolls |
| Sponges | 1 | 1 new | 6 |
| Laundry pods (guest washer) | 2 | 4 | 20 |
| AA and AAA batteries | rare | — | 4 each |
| Light bulbs (each type) | rare | — | 2 each |

### Reorder rule

**Reorder point = (usage per turnover × turnovers until delivery arrives) + 1 turnover of safety stock.**

Example: dishwasher tablets, 3 per turnover. You average 2 turnovers a week and delivery takes 5 days (about 1.5 turnovers). Reorder point = 3 × 1.5 + 3 = 7.5 → reorder when backstock hits 8.

Write the reorder point on a label on each backstock shelf. The cleaner counts backstock in every Turnover Report; you only act when a number reaches its label. Once a month, compare what you bought with turnovers done. If a consumable runs more than 30% above expected, guests are probably taking it: set out less and keep a visible spare instead.

## 5. Damage and missing items protocol

### Decision table

| Estimated cost | Action |
|---|---|
| Under €30 | Absorb, log it, replace. Not worth the review risk. |
| €30–150 | Send Template 5 and request payment through the platform's own resolution or payment-request tool. Never take payment off-platform. |
| Over €150, or any safety issue | Same message, then file a formal claim with the platform and/or apply any security deposit. Start the same day. |
| Smoking, unauthorized pets, parties | Apply the fee exactly as stated in your listing's house rules and quote the rule in the message. |

### Steps, in order

1. **Cleaner documents** (Section 2A): as-found video plus close-up and wide photos, before cleaning the area.
2. **Host finds the previous final photo set** showing the same item intact. That before photo is what makes most claims succeed, and it is why the 14-shot set is non-negotiable.
3. **Message the guest the same day** (Template 5). Facts, photos, cost. No adjectives.
4. **Get a quote or receipt within 72 hours.** For low-value items a link to the identical replacement with its price usually works; repairs need a written quote or invoice.
5. **File within the platform deadline.** Deadlines are short and terms change, so check your platform's current rules. Airbnb's host damage reimbursement has required submitting within 14 days of the guest's checkout, so start the request before the repair is finished.
6. **Evidence pack** (one folder per incident): as-found photos and video with timestamps, previous turnover's final photo of the item, receipt or quote, screenshot of the message thread, booking code.

### Lost and found

Bag the item, label it with booking code and date, photograph it, message the guest within 24 hours. Hold for 30 days. Guest pays shipping in advance through the platform. Cash, IDs, medication and electronics go into the locked owner closet immediately and never leave with the cleaner.

## 6. Deep-clean rotation

| Frequency | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Every turnover | Everything in Section 2, plus wiping switches, handles, remotes |
| Every 5th turnover | Baseboards, inside oven, wash shower curtain liner and bath mat, vacuum under beds and sofa, wipe inside kitchen cabinets, descale kettle |
| Monthly | Test smoke and CO alarms, descale coffee maker, wipe inside drawers, clean range hood filter if greasy, check fire extinguisher gauge |
| Quarterly | Wash duvets and pillows (or replace pillows), wash curtains, rotate mattresses, clean dryer vent duct, wash outside of windows, check under sinks for leaks |
| Yearly | Professional carpet and upholstery cleaning, replace mattress protectors and worn towels, clean and reseal grout, full inventory recount |

Track the turnover number for each property in the Turnover Report so the every-5th tasks are not forgotten. Pay an adder for them; they typically take 30–45 extra minutes.

## 7. Cleaner pay, quality and backup

**Flat rate formula:** flat fee per turnover = your measured average hours × local hourly cleaner rate × 1.1, plus fixed adders (on-site laundry, hot tub, every-5th deep tasks, same-day rush). Flat rates reward speed; the photo set protects quality.

**Quality score:** after each turnover, check 5 things from the photos: bed made correctly (shot 9), no hair in the shower (12), mirror clean (13), fridge empty (6), correct towel count (14). Score out of 5.

- 5/5: no action.
- 2 misses in 30 days: 10-minute call, walk through the photos together.
- Guest reports a cleaning miss at check-in: cleaner returns within 2 hours unpaid, or the cost of whoever fixes it is deducted. Agree this in writing at onboarding (Template 1), not after the first incident.

**Backup roster:** keep 2 backup cleaners who have each done at least one paid trial turnover at full rate. Give each backup one real turnover every 6–8 weeks so they stay familiar with the unit. Keep a one-page unit sheet for them: lockbox process, where backstock is, Wi-Fi, bin day, parking.

## 8. Message templates

Replace the [brackets]. Save them as quick replies on your booking platform or as text shortcuts on your phone.

### Template 1: Cleaner onboarding

> Hi [Name], here is how turnovers work at [Property]. 1) Before cleaning, take a 60–90 second video of the whole place as you found it. 2) Anything damaged, missing or unusual: send photos within 15 minutes, before cleaning that spot. 3) Follow the checklist here: [link]. 4) At the end, send the 14 photos from the same angles as the example album [link], plus the turnover report. I approve payment when the photos arrive. 5) Rate is [€X] per turnover, plus [€Y] for laundry and [€Z] for the every-5th-turnover deep tasks. 6) If a guest reports a cleaning miss at check-in, I'll ask you to come back within 2 hours to fix it, unpaid. Thanks, and tell me if anything in the checklist doesn't match the unit.

### Template 2: Cleaner issue report format

> ISSUE – [Property] – [date/time]
> What: [e.g., red wine stain on sofa, left cushion]
> Where: [room/location]
> Photos: [close-up + wide attached]
> Can I clean it? [yes / need product / no]
> Time impact: [+X min]

### Template 3: Guest pre-checkout reminder (send at 18:00 the evening before)

> Hi [Guest], hope you've enjoyed your stay! Checkout is tomorrow at [time]. Before you go: please run the dishwasher if there are dishes, put used towels in the bathtub, take rubbish to [location], and leave the keys [place]. No need to strip the beds. Safe travels!

### Template 4: Late checkout request

Yes:

> Hi [Guest], happy to give you until [time]. Please make sure you're out by then, as our cleaning team arrives right after. Enjoy the extra time!

No:

> Hi [Guest], I'd love to say yes, but we have guests arriving today and our cleaners need the full time to get the place ready. You're welcome to leave bags at [location / nearby luggage storage] until [time]. Thanks for understanding!

### Template 5: Damage or missing item

> Hi [Guest], thanks for staying with us. During cleaning after your checkout our team found [specific item and condition]. Photos are attached, alongside how it looked before your check-in. The [replacement/repair] cost is [€X] ([link or quote attached]). I'll send a request for this through [platform] so everything stays documented there. If there's anything I should know about what happened, please reply here. Thanks, [Name]

### Template 6: Guest cleanliness complaint at check-in

> Hi [Guest], I'm sorry about that, and thank you for telling me right away. Could you send a quick photo so I can share it with the cleaning team? I can have someone there within [time] to fix it, at a time that suits you. [If serious: I'd also like to offer (gesture).]

Then forward the guest's photo to the cleaner and compare it with their final photo set. If the final photo shows the problem, it is a cleaner miss; if it does not, log it without blame.

### Template 7: Backup cleaner call-out

> Hi [Name], can you take a turnover at [Property] on [date], [start time]–[must-finish time]? [Unit size], on-site laundry [yes/no], next guests arrive at [time]. Rate [€X]. Unit sheet and checklist: [link]. Please reply yes or no by [time] so I can line up someone else if needed.

- [ ] Set up a locked owner closet with labeled shelves and reorder points.
- [ ] Buy linens to par (Section 4).
- [ ] Send Template 1 to your cleaner and schedule a 30-minute walkthrough together.
- [ ] Save Templates 3–7 as quick replies on your booking platform.
- [ ] Recruit and trial 2 backup cleaners.
