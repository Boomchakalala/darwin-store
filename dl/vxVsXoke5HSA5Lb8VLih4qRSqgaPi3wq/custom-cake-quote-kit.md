# Custom Cake Pricing & Quote Kit for Home Bakers

Copy, fill in, send. Every number below is a starting point that you replace with your own after five tracked orders. Prices are in EUR; swap the symbol if you sell in GBP or USD, because the method is identical.

---

## 1. The Price Formula

**Cake price = Materials + Labour + Overhead + Profit**

| Line | How to calculate | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| Materials | Ingredient cost from your recipe x 1.10 (waste, test batches) + packaging (board, box, dowels, ribbon) | €12 to €40 per tier |
| Labour | All hours spent on the order x your hourly rate | €15 to €30 per hour |
| Overhead | Monthly fixed costs / cakes sold per month | €5 to €15 per cake |
| Profit | 15 to 25% of the three lines above | |

Round the final number **up** to the nearest €5.

### What counts as labour
Everything: answering messages, the consultation, shopping, baking, fillings, levelling, crumb coat, final coat, decorating, stacking, cleaning and delivery. If you only count decorating time, you are working half your hours for free.

### What counts as overhead
Extra oven energy, insurance, registration or licence fees, website and domain, payment-app fees, tool replacement (turntables, scrapers and tins wear out), photo props and advertising. Add up the yearly total, divide by 12, then divide by the number of cakes you sell in an average month.

### Worked example
8in round, 3 layers, vanilla with raspberry filling, smooth buttercream, piped border. Serves 24 wedding portions or 20 party portions.

| Line | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Ingredients | €14.00 x 1.10 | €15.40 |
| Packaging | board €1.50, box €2.50 | €4.00 |
| Labour | 5.5 h x €18 | €99.00 |
| Overhead | €120 per month / 12 cakes | €10.00 |
| Subtotal | | €128.40 |
| Profit 20% | | €25.68 |
| **Price** | rounded up | **€155** |

That works out to €6.46 per wedding serving. If you have been charging €60 for this cake, you have been earning about €6 an hour after ingredients, with nothing left for overhead or profit.

## 2. Time Benchmarks (hours)

Use these until you have your own data. Time your next five orders task by task with a phone timer, then replace the columns. A simple-finish 8in cake like the worked example sits between the first two columns.

| Task | 6in simple | 8in decorated | 2-tier (6in+8in) | 3-tier wedding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Messages, consult, quote | 0.5 | 0.5 | 1.0 | 2.0 |
| Shopping | 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.75 | 1.0 |
| Baking | 1.0 | 1.25 | 2.0 | 3.0 |
| Fillings and frosting | 0.75 | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.5 |
| Level, fill, crumb coat | 0.5 | 0.75 | 1.25 | 2.0 |
| Final coat | 0.5 | 0.75 | 1.5 | 2.5 |
| Decoration | 0.5 | 1.5 | 2.5 | 4.5 |
| Dowels and stacking | 0 | 0 | 0.5 | 1.0 |
| Clean-up | 0.5 | 0.75 | 1.0 | 1.5 |
| Handover or delivery | 0.25 | 0.25 | 0.5 | 1.5 |
| **Total** | **5.0** | **7.25** | **12.5** | **21.5** |

**Time savers that legitimately lower your price:** batch-bake and freeze sponges (baking 3 or more cakes at once roughly halves baking time per cake), offer a fixed flavour menu so fillings are routine, and sell standard sizes only.

## 3. Your Base Serving Rate

Price one standard cake with the formula, then divide by its servings. That number is your base serving rate.

Example: €155 / 24 = €6.46, rounded to a **€6.50 base**.

Apply a finish multiplier:

| Finish | Multiplier | At €6.50 base |
|---|---|---|
| Simple buttercream (smooth or rustic) | x 1.00 | €6.50 |
| Textured, piped or palette-knife buttercream | x 1.15 | €7.50 |
| Ganache with sharp edges | x 1.25 | €8.15 |
| Fondant covered | x 1.40 | €9.10 |
| Fondant with heavy hand detail | x 1.60 | €10.40 |

**Quote = servings x serving rate + add-ons + delivery, rounded up to €5.**

Example: 2-tier ganache cake, 36 wedding servings: 36 x €8.15 = €293.40, plus a drip at €12 and 18 km delivery at €18 = €323.40. Quote €325.

### The small-cake trap
Small cakes take almost as long as medium ones, so per-serving pricing underprices them. A 6in cake at €6.50 per serving is €78, but the formula with 5 hours of work gives about €130. Always apply your minimum order (section 6): **price = whichever is higher, serving price or minimum order.**

## 5. Serving Charts

Party serving: roughly 1.5in x 2in slice of a 4in-tall cake. Wedding serving: roughly 1in x 2in slice of a 4in-tall cake. **Always state which one you mean in the quote.**

| Size | Round party | Round wedding | Square party | Square wedding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6in | 12 | 12 | 12 | 18 |
| 8in | 20 | 24 | 20 | 32 |
| 10in | 28 | 38 | 30 | 50 |
| 12in | 40 | 56 | 48 | 72 |
| 14in | 63 | 78 | 63 | 98 |

**Common round tier stacks (wedding servings):** 6+8 = 36, 6+8+10 = 74, 8+10+12 = 118, 6+8+10+12 = 130.

**Extended tiers:** for 6in-tall tiers, multiply servings by 1.5.

**Decision rules when a customer gives a headcount:**
- Birthday or party, no other dessert: use party servings and go to the next size up if the count lands between sizes.
- Wedding with a separate dessert: wedding servings are fine.
- Wedding where the cake is the dessert: use party servings, or add a cutting cake in the kitchen.

## 6. Minimum Order and Payment Terms

**Minimum order:** the formula price of your smallest standard cake. With the example numbers that is €110 to €135 for a 6in cake. Many bakers set €90 to €120 and accept a thinner margin on small cakes, or stop selling 6in cakes as stand-alone orders. Either is fine. Pricing below materials plus 3 hours of labour is not.

**Payment schedule:**

| Order type | At booking | Balance due | Design locked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Celebration cake | 50% booking fee | 7 days before | 7 days before |
| Wedding or order over €500 | 30% booking fee | 14 days before | 30 days before |

Two fixed rules: no date is held without the booking payment, and no cake leaves without full payment.

Rules on deposits, cancellations and selling food made at home vary by country and region. Check your local requirements before publishing your policy.

## 7. Intake Questions

Send these as your first reply or put them in a form. Do not quote until you have answers to 1 to 7.

1. Event date, and the time the cake is needed
2. Collection or delivery (full address if delivery)
3. Number of guests, and whether other desserts are served
4. Flavour(s) from the menu
5. Allergies or dietary needs in the group
6. Design idea: up to 3 photos, plus colours
7. Wording, name or age on the cake
8. Budget range: under €100 / €100 to €200 / €200 to €400 / over €400
9. How did you find me?

Asking for budget lets you design to the price instead of quoting blind and getting ghosted.

## 8. Message Scripts

Replace the [brackets] and send.

**8.1 First reply**
> Hi [name], thanks for getting in touch! I'd love to make your cake. So I can give you an accurate quote, could you answer these quick questions? [paste section 7]. Once I have these, I'll send your quote within [24/48] hours.

**8.2 Sending the quote**
> Hi [name], here's your quote for [date]: [description], serving [number] [party/wedding] portions, total €[price]. A [50/30]% booking fee of €[amount] secures your date; the balance is due [7/14] days before. I only take [number] orders per weekend, so dates are confirmed in the order booking fees arrive. Would you like me to hold [date] for you?

**8.3 That's more than I expected**
> Totally understand. Custom cakes are priced on time as well as ingredients, and this design takes around [hours] hours. If you'd like to bring the cost down, I can [option A: switch to buttercream, saving €X] or [option B: one tier plus a kitchen cutting cake, saving €X]. Happy to requote either one.

**8.4 Can you do it cheaper?**
> My prices are the same for every customer, so I can't discount the design as it stands. What I can do is adjust the design to fit your budget. What figure did you have in mind? I'll show you what's possible at that price.

**8.5 Friends and family**
> I'd love to make this for you! For friends I [waive the delivery fee / include a free flavour upgrade]. The cake itself is €[price], because the hours are the same whoever it's for.

**8.6 Last-minute request**
> I can fit this in for [date]. Because it's less than [7 days / 72 hours] away, there's a rush fee of [25/50]%, making the total €[price]. Full payment confirms it.

**8.7 Copying another baker's photo**
> Lovely inspiration! I'll create my own version in the same style rather than an exact copy, and I'll send you a quick sketch or description before the date. Your quote below is based on [main elements].

**8.8 Follow-up after 48 hours with no reply**
> Hi [name], just checking you received your quote for [date]. I've had another enquiry for that weekend, so let me know if you'd like me to hold it. No worries if your plans have changed!

Only mention another enquiry if it is true. Send one follow-up, never two.

**8.9 Declining**
> Thank you for thinking of me! Unfortunately I'm [fully booked / not able to do this design / not delivering that far] on [date]. I hope your celebration is wonderful.

**8.10 Change request after booking**
> No problem, I can make that change. It adjusts the total by €[amount], so the new balance is €[amount]. Please confirm by [date], as the design locks [7/30] days before the event.

## 10. Policy Block (copy-paste and adapt)

**Booking.** Your date is reserved once the booking fee is paid. The booking fee is non-refundable because it holds a date I turn other orders away for.

**Payment.** The balance is due [7/14] days before your event. Unpaid orders are cancelled at that point and the booking fee is kept.

**Changes.** Design, flavour and size changes are welcome until [7/30] days before the event and may change the price. After that, only wording changes are possible.

**Cancellation.** More than [30] days' notice: your booking fee can be moved to a new date within 6 months, once. Less than [30] days: the booking fee is kept. Less than [7] days: the full amount is due, as ingredients and time are already committed.

**Allergens.** My kitchen handles [gluten, eggs, dairy, nuts, soy]. I take care to avoid cross-contact but cannot guarantee any product is allergen-free.

**Collection and delivery.** Responsibility for the cake passes to you at collection. Keep it flat, cool and out of direct sun. For deliveries, responsibility passes once the cake is set up and photographed at the venue.

**Inedible items.** Dowels, wires, toppers and some flower stems must be removed before serving.

**Photos.** I may photograph your cake for my portfolio. Tell me at booking if you'd prefer I don't.

## 11. When to Raise Prices

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Turning away 2 or more orders a week for 6 weeks | +10% across the board |
| Ingredient basket up more than 8% since your last calculation | Recalculate materials, adjust prices |
| Every January | Rerun the formula with your real tracked hours |
| Booking 3 months ahead consistently | +10% on weekend dates |

**Announcement script:**
> From [date], my prices are updating to reflect ingredient costs and the time each design takes. Any order booked before [date] keeps its current price, so if you have a celebration coming up, now is a great time to secure your date.
