Questions

What format is it?

A single Markdown document you download instantly. It opens in any text editor or notes app, and the tables, scripts and quote template can be copied straight into your messages.

I'm a beginner. Should I really charge these prices?

The kit gives you a method, not fixed prices. You plug in your own ingredient costs, hourly rate and overhead, and the time benchmarks are replaced by your own tracked hours after five orders.

I don't sell in euros. Does it still work?

Yes. The formula, multipliers and percentages work in any currency. Simply swap the symbol and adjust the example amounts to your local costs.

Does it cover cupcakes, cookies or desserts?

No. The time benchmarks, serving charts and add-on list are built for custom celebration and tiered cakes. The core formula applies to any bake, but the rest is cake-specific.

Does it tell me if I'm allowed to sell from home?

No. Home food business rules vary by country and region. The kit reminds you to check local requirements, but it does not give legal or licensing advice.
